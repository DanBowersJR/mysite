# The Hidden Risks of Anonymous Student Surveys

## Introduction

Student surveys are important tools for improving education. They allow institutions to collect feedback and identify courses that may need improvement. However, collecting this information raises ethical concerns, particularly when students are promised anonymity.

In this assignment, I will examine a student survey that requires participants to log in using their usernames and passwords while claiming that their responses are anonymous.

## Ethical Concerns

One major concern is privacy. Even though the survey does not ask students for their names, requiring them to log in could potentially allow their responses to be linked to their identities.

This raises questions about transparency, informed consent, confidentiality, and data protection.

## Recommendations

Educational institutions should clearly explain what information is collected, how it is stored, and who can access it. They should also ensure that survey responses cannot be traced back to individual students if anonymity is promised.

## Conclusion

Data analysts have a responsibility to protect participants and ensure that collected information is used ethically. Maintaining trust is just as important as obtaining useful data.
